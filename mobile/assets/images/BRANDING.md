# Karunya Media Desk Branding

Updated on 24 October 2026.

## Identity: Broadcast Microphone & Concentric Soundwaves
- **Concept**: A modern minimalist icon featuring a studio broadcast microphone encircled by concentric expanding soundwave frequencies within a rounded squircle container. It communicates live event coverage, audio-visual technical operations, chapel services, and media broadcasting.
- **Color Palette**:
  - **Royal Cobalt Blue** (`#01358B` / `rgb(1, 53, 139)`): Primary app background and icon badge.
  - **Clean White** (`#FFFFFF`): Geometric microphone line art and concentric ripples.
- **Assets**:
  - `icon.png` / `media-ops-icon.png`: 1024 × 1024 px full-bleed royal blue launcher icon for iOS and standard Expo builds.
  - `media-ops-mark.png`: 1024 × 1024 px squircle badge with transparent corner margins, used for the Sign-In screen header and native splash screen.
  - `android-icon-foreground.png`: 1024 × 1024 px transparent canvas with the white microphone and soundwave symbol scaled into Android's circular safe zone.
  - `android-icon-background.png`: 1024 × 1024 px matching `#01358B` solid background.
  - `android-icon-monochrome.png`: White silhouette for Android 13+ Material You themed icons.
  - `splash-icon.png`: 512 × 512 px squircle badge.
  - `favicon.png`: 192 × 192 px web favicon.
